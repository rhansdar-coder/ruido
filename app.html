The instrument

How many notes could you be?

Two numbers, and the gap between them is the product. Claimed is the set the pool advertises. Effective is the set that survives an adversary who knows the block and reads a public RPC. Ruido measures the second, and sells cover traffic to widen it.

What does it cost to buy a bit?

A calibrated model, not a promise. Change the parameters and watch the price of anonymity move.

Parameters

Read this first

Bits is log2 of the candidate set. One more bit doubles the number of notes you could be. Under one bit, the adversary is guessing between you and one other note.

Blind is averaged over the whole pool because it helps everyone a little. Window-only and aimed are measured on the buyer's note, because that is the only note they help. Switch between the three at a fixed decoy count and watch the cost per bit: the gap between them is the price of what the provider is told.
